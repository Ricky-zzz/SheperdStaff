# Shepherd Staff — Demo Video Script & Flow

Target length: ~2.5 minutes (screen demo only; problem animation to be added later).
Record screen first, voiceover after. Airplane mode is fine — the app is fully offline (worth mentioning in VO).

## Scene 1 — Home (0:00–0:20)

**On screen:** Open app, hold on dashboard ~5s, scroll slowly once.

**VO:** "This is Shepherd Staff, my offline farm record app. From the home screen I see today's whole farm: my animals, feed running low, and today's tasks."

## Scene 2 — Livestock, Betty (0:20–0:45)

**On screen:** Tap Livestock → tap Betty the cow → scroll her health and feeding history.

**VO:** "Every animal has one record. Betty is a dairy cow. Her breed, location, health notes, and every feeding are all here — no more flipping through ledger pages."

## Scene 3 — Record feeding (0:45–1:15)

**On screen:** Tap Record feeding on Betty → pick feeds, enter 2 kilos → save → show updated history and stock.

**VO:** "I record this morning's feeding: two kilos of feeds for Betty. The app deducts it from stock and attaches the cost to her automatically. That link between feed and animal is what paper can't do."

## Scene 4 — Expense (1:15–1:35)

**On screen:** Expenses → New → fence repair, 1500 → save → show in list.

**VO:** "Money out works the same way. Fence repair, fifteen hundred pesos, logged in seconds and categorized."

## Scene 5 — Income (1:35–1:55)

**On screen:** Income → egg sale, 30 eggs → save → show in list.

**VO:** "And money in. Thirty eggs sold this morning. Same list, same day, one picture of cash flow."

## Scene 6 — Reports, the payoff (1:55–2:25)

**On screen:** Reports → profit/loss → feed cost breakdown → linger ~5s on each.

**VO:** "Here's why it matters. The report shows income against expenses, and exactly how much feed is eating into profit. A ledger never tells you this."

## Scene 7 — Tasks + Backup closer (2:25–2:50)

**On screen:** Tasks → complete daily feeding → More → Backup → tap Back up now.

**VO:** "Daily work is tracked too — today's feeding, done. And everything is backed up to a file I can email myself, so a lost phone doesn't mean lost records. That's Shepherd Staff."

## Recording notes

- Seed data: Betty, Ben, goats, chickens, pigs (fresh install + onboarding to seed).
- Kill notifications before recording; use slow deliberate taps (~2x normal speed).
- One clean take per scene; trim pauses in CapCut.
- Record VO afterwards over the trimmed footage, not live.
